Open rejects zero limit/null callback. feed(NULL,0) is a no-op on a healthy stream.
feed/finish: 0 success, negative failure. Successful finish is idempotent; subsequent
nonempty feed is an error. Callback returns 0 on success, nonzero to fail permanently.
flags is the resolved ISO sample_flags word, not a boolean keyframe flag.
DTS must fit uint64_t and PTS int64_t, otherwise fail rather than wrap.
No allocation may scale with total media length: limit independently bounds retained
init data, all current-moof tables (including derived sample descriptions) and one sample.
Only complete valid init may be published to the fragment state; use existing MP4D
configuration parsing rather than another codec-config decoder. Factor internals if needed.
Independent fragments may be sparse by track. No overlapping runs, no external data,
no zero-length boxes, encryption, multiple sample descriptions or implicit tfhd base.
